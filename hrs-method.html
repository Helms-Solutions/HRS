The framework

Retirement protection is bigger than picking one policy.

The HRS Method helps organize the questions that affect your healthcare, income, assets, family, and long-term confidence.

H

Health

Medicare strategy, prescriptions, doctors, hospital exposure, critical illness, dental, vision, hearing, and long-term care concerns.

R

Retirement

Income needs, inflation, taxes, market exposure, emergency access, and the risk of outliving retirement assets.

S

Security

Family protection, final expenses, beneficiaries, estate preparation, long-term care planning, and legacy goals.

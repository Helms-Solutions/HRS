The HRS Method

Retirement protection is bigger than picking a plan.

Most decisions are handled separately. The HRS Method brings the three areas together so you can make clearer, more coordinated choices.

H

Health

Medicare, prescriptions, doctors, hospital exposure, critical illness, dental, vision, hearing, and long-term care concerns.

R

Retirement

Income, inflation, taxes, market exposure, emergency access, and the risk of outliving retirement assets.

S

Security

Family protection, final expenses, beneficiaries, estate preparation, long-term care planning, and legacy goals.
